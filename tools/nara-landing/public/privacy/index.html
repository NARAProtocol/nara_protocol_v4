Privacy Policy & Data Minimization Notice

Effective & Last Updated: November 19, 2026 · Applies to naraprotocol.io (and naraprotocol.com), slip.naraprotocol.io, cryptoslip.eth.limo & official protocol bots

Plain-English Summary: NARA Protocol and CryptoSLIP are open-source, non-custodial software interfaces with no corporate entity, no user accounts, no KYC forms, no password databases, no advertising pixels, and zero tracking cookies. Private keys and payment-link secrets remain strictly on your device or inside client-side URL # fragments (RFC 3986) that browsers never transmit to servers.

The public web interfaces at https://naraprotocol.io (including /vault and /research/*) and https://slip.naraprotocol.io (alongside decentralized IPFS/ENS mirrors such as https://cryptoslip.eth.limo) are published and maintained by independent NARA Protocol & CryptoSLIP Open-Source Contributors (“Contributors,” “we,” or “us”) without a centralized corporate custodian.

2. Zero Tracking Cookies & Strictly Necessary Device Storage (ePrivacy / PECR)

We do not set advertising, cross-site tracking, behavioral profiling, or third-party marketing cookies (Set-Cookie is unused across our interfaces; no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, client-side analytics beacons, or Google Fonts CDN is loaded — all web fonts are self-hosted locally and HTML responses send Cache-Control: no-transform). When you open the optional Fame Board submission modal, Cloudflare Turnstile (challenges.cloudflare.com) may place strictly necessary anti-bot security tokens solely to block automated spam (exempt under EU ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) / UK PECR Reg. 6). Because zero non-essential cookies or behavioral trackers are used, we do not interrupt you with deceptive cookie-consent popups.

3. Public Blockchain Data vs. Off-Chain Edge Data

Public Base Mainnet Data (Chain ID 8453): When you broadcast a swap, Vault Receipt purchase, NARA commitment, or CryptoSLIP voucher transaction on the Base blockchain, your public 0x wallet address, token amounts, and smart-contract events (including NARAPositionNFTV5 and CryptoSlipNFTV32 ERC-721 records) are permanently recorded on a public, decentralized ledger. Open-source contributors do not control Base validators, block explorers, or public archive nodes and cannot edit, hide, or erase on-chain blockchain history.

URL # Fragment Secrets: CryptoSLIP payment links keep ephemeral claim keys or salts inside the URL hash fragment (after #). Under RFC 3986 § 3.5, browsers strip the # fragment before sending HTTP requests, and our client immediately clears the fragment from your address bar on load.

Because decentralized apps read blockchain state directly from your browser without a centralized middleman database, your browser may establish direct HTTPS connections (which inherently transmit your IP address at the TCP/TLS layer) to:

  • Base Mainnet RPC & Bundler Nodes: dRPC, mainnet.base.org, Tenderly, PublicNode, LlamaRPC, 1RPC, Pocket Network, OnFinality, and (when using Circle USDC gas fallback) Pimlico and Candide. In CryptoSLIP (About → Base node mesh), you may configure your own private HTTPS Base node URL.

  • Market Data & Non-NARA Bridge Quotes: Read-only price/route endpoints (CoinGecko, DexScreener, KyberSwap, LI.FI).
  • Telegram Mini App SDK & Optional Payment On-Ramps: telegram.org (telegram-web-app.js loaded with referrerpolicy="no-referrer" for Telegram Mini App compatibility) and optional user-initiated external services such as MoonPay (buy.moonpay.com) or peer transfer links, which operate under their own independent privacy policies and KYC terms when you choose to open them.

We never sell, rent, or broker personal data (CCPA/CPRA: zero sale and zero cross-context behavioral sharing of personal information in the preceding 12 months). We honor browser Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals automatically by maintaining zero sale or cross-context sharing for every visitor by default, and we never discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.
