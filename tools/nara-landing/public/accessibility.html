Accessibility Statement

Last Reviewed: November 19, 2026 · Covers naraprotocol.io (and naraprotocol.com), slip.naraprotocol.io & cryptoslip.eth.limo

1. Commitment & Conformance Target

NARA Protocol & CryptoSLIP Open-Source Contributors are committed to ensuring that self-custodial Web3 tools work reliably for everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, mobile zoom, and reduced-motion preferences. We design and test our public web interfaces against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA and European standard EN 301 549 (aligned with the European Accessibility Act).

2. Implemented Accessibility Measures

  • Keyboard & Focus Navigation: Skip-to-main-content links on top-level pages, visible high-contrast focus outlines (focus-visible), logical tab order, and Escape key dismissal across all modals and bottom sheets.
  • Touch Targets & Viewport Zoom: Primary interactive controls meet 40px–48px minimum touch targets; pinch-to-zoom and text scaling are never disabled (maximum-scale / user-scalable=no are banned), and form inputs in CryptoSLIP use ≥ 16px font sizes to prevent unintended mobile viewport jumps.
  • Screen-Reader Semantics & Live Announcements: Semantic landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>), ARIA modal roles (role="dialog", aria-modal="true"), programmatic input labels, and polite live regions (role="status", aria-live="polite") for quote updates, clipboard copies, and transaction progress.
  • Color, Contrast & Non-Color Indicators: High-contrast obsidian dark surfaces paired with text labels, symbols, and monospaced tabular numerals so status is never conveyed by color alone (WCAG 1.4.1 & 1.4.3).
  • Audio & Motion Control: Sound effects include explicit one-tap mute controls, victory and decorative motion effects honor the OS prefers-reduced-motion setting, and layouts respect device viewport and safe-area constraints.

3. Known Third-Party Limitations

External wallet extensions and mobile wallet browsers (such as MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, or Phantom), Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot challenges, and external block explorers (BaseScan, DexScreener) render their own third-party interfaces outside our codebase. Where an injected wallet popup presents barriers, CryptoSLIP provides a built-in browser wallet with WebAuthn passkeys and a standalone plain-HTML offline recovery page (recover-wallet.html).

4. Feedback & Barrier Reporting

If you encounter any accessibility barrier, screen-reader issue, or keyboard trap on naraprotocol.io or slip.naraprotocol.io, please email contact@naraprotocol.io with the page URL and assistive technology used. Open-source contributors review and aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 10 business days.
