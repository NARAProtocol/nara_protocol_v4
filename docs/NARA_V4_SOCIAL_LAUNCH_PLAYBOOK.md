## 1. Strategy in one page

**Funnel:** Attention → Trust → Invite.

1. **Attention** — post a contrarian opinion about the current market, not a
   NARA fact. (The skill rule: *trend first, NARA second.*)
2. **Trust** — show receipts, not adjectives: the team committed before the
   public, the reserve is sealed, the supply is fixed, the ETH flows on-chain.
3. **Invite** — one soft nudge to the site. Never hard-sell.

**The identity to build:** NARA = *proof of patience*. Every post should make
the reader feel either "that's me" (the burned OG) or "that's different"
(the skeptic). Never "that's another yield farm."

**What we are NOT selling:** safety, APY, guarantees, "the next 100x." Those
are banned by our own rules and they convert the wrong crowd anyway.

## 2. Crypto X user profiles (who we're writing to)

### 2.1 The Burned OG — PRIMARY TARGET
- **Who:** 28–45. Held through 2017 and/or 2021. Made money once, gave it back
  later. Still checks the market, posts "I remember when…" takes.
- **Wants:** to be early on something real again; to not feel stupid for
  holding.
- **Fears:** being the exit liquidity one more time.
- **Trigger:** "you won't be the exit here, because the game doesn't work that
  way here."
- **Action:** convert. This is the core audience. Every post is written for them.

### 2.2 The Degen — DO NOT SPEND BUDGET
- **Who:** churns memecoins, refresh-button hands, lives on the chart.
- **Wants:** dopamine, now.
- **Fears:** missing the pump.
- **Trigger:** none. They will not commit to a year. But they *will* amplify a
  sharp, funny, contrarian post if it's quotable.
- **Action:** write for them only as a distribution channel, never as the buyer.

### 2.3 The Builder — HIGH VALUE, LOW VOLUME
- **Who:** ships contracts, reads the code, respects sound mechanics.
- **Wants:** a novel, correctly-engineered primitive.
- **Fears:** marketing fluff, unaudited spaghetti.
- **Trigger:** the Uniswap v4 hook math, `notifyEthRewards()` being
  permissionless, the per-block pressure curve.
- **Action:** win them with mechanic posts (§5 angle: mechanic). Builders
  legitimize you to everyone else.

### 2.4 The Curious Lurker — TERTIARY
- **Who:** new-ish to crypto, reads threads, never posts, DMs friends.
- **Wants:** to understand, to not get scammed.
- **Fears:** jargon, complexity.
- **Trigger:** the leveling NFT visual — "your position physically changes as
  it earns."
- **Action:** convert later, once there's a simple UI. Keep one post a week
  that a newcomer can understand.

### 2.5 The Skeptic — DEFENSIVE TARGET
- **Who:** will quote-tweet "scam" on sight. Knows enough to be dangerous.
- **Wants:** to be right about it being a rug.
- **Fears:** being wrong and missing something real.
- **Trigger:** receipts. Team committed first. Reserve sealed and verifiable.
  Fixed supply, no mint.
- **Action:** do not argue. Point to on-chain evidence. A calm reply with a
  receipt converts the *audience*, even if it never converts the skeptic.

### 2.6 The Base Native — CHANNEL-SPECIFIC
- **Who:** active on Base, follows the ecosystem, likes onchain novelty and
  receipts.
- **Wants:** credible Base-native projects, not bridged marketing.
- **Fears:** another chain's leftovers re-skinned.
- **Trigger:** "native to Base," on-chain verifiable, real ETH flows.
- **Action:** convert. Tag Base ecosystem accounts on genuinely Base-native
  posts (§7).

## 3. Voice, vocabulary, and anti-AI rules

### 3.1 Voice (from the `nara-social` skill)
- The poster is a **builder who got burned twice** — once as a holder, once as
  a builder using the same broken model. NARA exists because of that.
- Tone: **matter-of-fact, dry, not angry.** Assumes the reader is smart.
  Comfortable being wrong. Never moralizes. Never sounds like a product page.
- **Opinion first, NARA second.** Never open with a NARA fact.

### 3.2 Anti-AI-tell rules (apply to every draft)
- No "Not X. Not Y. An actual Z." triple negation.
- No em dashes as connectors. Use periods.
- No "Worth knowing:" / "Worth understanding:" openers.
- No closing summary sentence ("That's the mechanic you're committing to.").
- No capitalized protocol nouns: engine, reserve, protocol, grid → lowercase.
- No numbered use-case lists in a post.
- Lowercase throughout. Vary length: some posts 2 lines, some 8.
- Read it out loud. If it sounds like a product page, rewrite it.

### 3.3 Vocabulary map (say / never say)

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| commit / commitment | lock / stake (user-facing) |
| committed participants | lockers / stakers |
| commitment duration / weight | lock duration / stake weight |
| duration earns structurally more | higher APY / better yield |
| sealed reward budget / closed budget | guaranteed rewards |
| fixed supply | inflationary emissions |
| ETH from fees flows in | passive income |
| receipts / on-chain record | "trust us" |
| base-native | (nothing — just be accurate) |

### 3.4 The banned list (from `V4_POSITIONING.md`)
Never: "v4 is live" (until the full release is announced). Never publish
addresses, thresholds, timing, allocations, or bond-opening conditions before
the official announcement. Never say bonds, baskets, stNARA, or the Position
NFT are live until each has its own verified manifest. Never claim "safe,"
"risk free," "guaranteed," "recommended," "best," or projected returns.

## 4. Posting times (real windows, UTC)

Crypto is 24/7 and global. The highest-quality engagement clusters around US
East + Asia/Europe overlap. Default rhythm: **3 posts a day.**

| Slot | UTC | US East (EDT) | Kyiv (UTC+3) | Why |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Slot 1 | **13:00** | 9:00 am | 16:00 | US morning, Europe afternoon |
| Slot 2 | **17:00** | 1:00 pm | 20:00 | US midday, EU evening |
| Slot 3 | **22:00** | 6:00 pm | 01:00 | US East evening peak |
| Slot 4 (test) | **01:00** | 9:00 pm | 04:00 | Asia morning, US night |

- **Weekdays:** run all three slots.
- **Sunday:** long-form thread at 17:00 UTC (threads get read on Sundays).
- **Saturday:** lighter, one or two posts, more replies.
- **Rule:** post manually for the first 2 weeks (no scheduler). The engagement
  log (§11) tells you which slot your specific audience is actually awake for.

## 8. Reply & engagement strategy

- **Reply to trending threads** with a standing opinion, then one NARA line,
  then a link. (Example in the `nara-social` skill: reply to a hot take, don't
  shill cold.)
- **Never shill in unrelated threads.** It kills credibility with the exact
  audience you're targeting.
- **Reply to skeptics with receipts, not arguments.** One calm reply with
  on-chain evidence converts the audience even when it doesn't convert the
  skeptic.
- **Quote-tweet your own winners.** When a post gets traction, quote it with
  the next layer of the story the next day.
- **Engage back within 30 minutes** of posting. The algorithm rewards
  responsive accounts in the first hour.

## 10. Compliance guardrails (read before every post)

1. Neutral action hierarchy — never `recommended`, `best`, `safest`,
   `popular`, `trending`, `highest return`, or any phrasing that picks an asset
   for the reader.
2. No yield, APY, return, or price promises. Ever.
3. No "safe", "risk free", "guaranteed", "protected", "insured".
4. No addresses, thresholds, timing, allocation numbers, or bond-opening
   conditions before the official announcement.
5. Don't claim a surface is live until it is: Position NFT, bonds, baskets,
   stNARA are not live yet. Say "the fixed-v4 core is on Base", nothing more.
6. Every factual claim must trace to a receipt or `CURRENT_STATE.md`.

## 11. KPIs and the learning loop

Track per post (the `nara-social` skill logs to an engagement file):

- angle used (opinion / mechanic / contrast / update / hook / alpha)
- replies, quotes, reposts, likes
- what the first reply was about

**Read the loop:** which angles pull builders vs degens vs lurkers. Which
opinions start debates. Do more of what starts debate.

**30-day milestones:**
- Week 1: profile live, founding story posted, Sunday thread pinned.
- Week 2: mechanic + proof posts; first skeptic handled with receipts.
- Week 3: UGC begins (position flex posts); first quote-retweet by a builder.
- Week 4: one substantial thread per week; Base ecosystem engagement; review
  the log and pick the winning angle.

**Success is not "viral."** Success in week 4 is: a handful of builders
quoting the mechanic, a handful of burned OGs saying "this is for me," and a
log that tells you which angle compounds. Virality is downstream of that.
